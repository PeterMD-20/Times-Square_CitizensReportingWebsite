Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

What can I report?

Community problems such as damaged roads, uncollected garbage, broken street lights, flooding, noise, and safety hazards.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Creating an account lets us contact you about your report.

What should I include?

A clear subject, exact location, description, and a photo or video if possible.

How do I track my report?

Enter your reference number on the Track page to see its details and progress.

What do the statuses mean?

Submitted, Received, Under Review, Assigned, In Progress, and Resolved.

Is this a real service?

No. This is a class prototype. Reports and sample statuses are demonstration data.

Privacy Policy

Your information is used only to process and update your report.

Data Protection

Uploaded Files
